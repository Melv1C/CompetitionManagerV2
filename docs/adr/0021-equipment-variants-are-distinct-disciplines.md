# Equipment variants are distinct Disciplines

A Discipline identifies the specifications under which an athletics result is produced. Different hurdle heights or spacing and implement weights are separate Disciplines, because sharing one identity would mix results produced under different rules and make external event mappings ambiguous. This extends the legacy catalogue's variant boundaries while allowing each variant to have its own stable code and translations.

Once a Discipline has been used by a Competition Event, its identity and specifications remain fixed. A changed specification creates a new Discipline; the old one may be deactivated for future selection while existing Events and results retain their reference.
