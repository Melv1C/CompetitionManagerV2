# Event revenue belongs to the Organization

The Organization receives exactly the Event Entry prices collected for its Competition after the configured settlement delay. A payment-method-neutral Checkout Fee, calculated once per paid cart from a platform-wide fixed EUR amount plus a percentage and snapshotted before submission, is retained by the platform; Organization-managed reimbursements occur outside this ledger. Its presentation and formula require Belgian/EU legal review before production and must not be represented as a card surcharge.
